Privacy Policy

What stays on your iPhone

Ringside is a boxing round timer. Your timer settings, presets and preferences are stored on your device. We have no servers and no accounts, and we cannot see this data.

Workout video

If you turn on recording, Ringside uses the camera and microphone to record your rounds. Each video is saved directly to your Photos library and nowhere else. Ringside does not upload, store or share your videos, and it never reads your existing photos or videos. You can turn recording off in Settings, or remove access in iOS Settings → Ringside.

Notifications and Live Activities

Ringside can send local reminders and show a Live Activity so the timer stays visible. These are created on your device and do not involve our servers.

Ads

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. Before ads, you may see a consent form (Google UMP) and, on iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, coarse location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, and may use it for tracking if you allow it. We never give Google your workouts or videos. Ringside never shows ads during a workout or while recording. You can change your choice in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices (where available), or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See how Google uses data.

Purchases

If you buy Remove Ads, Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your payment details. Buying it turns off ads.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove its data from your device. Videos saved to Photos stay in Photos until you delete them there.
