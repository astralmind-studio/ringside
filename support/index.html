Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email astralminds.studio@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Recording stopped when I locked my phone

iOS stops the camera when an app leaves the screen. Keep Ringside open while recording. The timer keeps running, and the part already recorded is saved.

My music stopped when I turned on recording

Music pauses while Record Workouts is on, because the camera and microphone take over the iPhone's audio. Turn recording off to train with music; the bells play over it.

Where are my videos?

In the Photos app. Ringside saves one clip per round, starting and stopping on the bell. Rest periods aren't recorded.

The bells didn't ring with my phone locked

Check that the ring/silent switch and volume are up, and that notifications for Ringside are allowed in iOS Settings → Ringside → Notifications. They act as a backup bell if iOS closes the app.

I bought Remove Ads on another device

Open Settings in Ringside and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I change ad privacy choices?

Use Settings → Ad Privacy Choices in Ringside (shown where required), or iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
